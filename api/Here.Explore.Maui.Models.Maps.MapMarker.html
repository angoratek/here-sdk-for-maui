Remarks

AnchorX/AnchorY position the image relative to the coordinates, as percentages of the image size (0–100): 50/50 centers the image on the coordinate, 50/100 puts the bottom-center there (typical pin). Anchoring is applied on Android; the iOS bridge does not expose it.
